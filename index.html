FixKeep Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Overview

FixKeep is an offline-first Android app for saving warranty records, receipt photos, repair history, and reminder dates. This release is designed to keep your data on your device unless Android backup or device transfer is enabled by the device owner.

Data you save in the app

  • Item names, brands, categories, and model details
  • Purchase dates, sellers, prices, and notes
  • Warranty lengths and expiry dates
  • Repair history, technician notes, and parts replaced
  • Reminder schedules and custom reminder messages
  • Receipt photos you choose to attach

How FixKeep uses your data

FixKeep uses your saved information only to display your records in the app and create on-device reminders for warranty or service dates.

Data sharing and network use

This release does not require an account, does not include cloud sync, and does not send your saved records to a developer-operated backend. Google Play Integrity may be configured in release builds for app security signals, but integrity setup does not upload your saved item content.

Backup and retention

If Android device backup or device transfer is enabled on your device, app data may be included in that system-managed process. You can delete records inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local app sandbox data from the device.

Security

FixKeep relies on Android app sandboxing, local device storage protections, and optional Google Play Integrity configuration for release hardening. Integrity checks do not replace server-side verification for paid or security-sensitive features.

Contact

For app support, please use the contact details provided on the FixKeep Google Play listing.
